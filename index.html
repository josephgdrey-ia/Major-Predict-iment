📖 How to Play & Scoring

Points are awarded based on cumulative match wins for the players you draft. The deeper a player goes in Flushing Meadows, the more points each subsequent win is worth. Tie-breaker will be first total sets won, followed by points won, followed by first serve percentage.

  • Round 1 Win (1 win) +1 pt
  • Round 2 Win (2 wins) +2 pts (3 total)
  • Round 3 Win (3 wins) +3 pts (6 total)
  • Round 4 Win (4 wins) +4 pts (10 total)
  • Quarterfinal Win (5 wins) +5 pts (15 total)
  • Semifinal Win (6 wins) +6 pts (21 total)
  • Championship Win (7 wins) +7 pts (28 total)

🧾 Roster Rules

Every manager drafts 8 players — 4 men and 4 women, one from each ranking tier per gender. No doubling up inside a tier.

📈 What "Ceiling" Means

Your ceiling is the highest score you could still finish with. Only one player can win each draw, so your surviving men are slotted into the best finishes still available — champion (28), runner-up (21), then two semifinal spots (15) — and your women the same. It ignores the possibility that two of your own players meet each other, so treat it as a generous best case. Once your ceiling falls below the leader's current score, you're mathematically done.
